# Challenge Faced
So far, some of the challenges that we have faced are that it is not easy to create a project out of thin air. It was also a daunting task simply because we had to review what we knew about computer architecture and shuffle through all the files in order to even begin to understand what exactly we are being asked to do in terms of this project. We also had way too many ideas when it came to what exactly we should create and it was difficult to settle down into a singular idea that we think could solve the design request as specified in this competition. Finally the last challenge that we faced was to relinquish the creative design power to AI and their capabilities. While we understand that at the end of the day we get to change the errors that AI makes, we still found it difficult to relinquish that power because it meant that AI could potentially produce algorithms that we were not familiar with, at least when it comes to formatting and actual execution of them.

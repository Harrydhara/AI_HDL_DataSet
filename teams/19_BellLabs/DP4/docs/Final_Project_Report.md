# AI-HDL 2026: Comprehensive Project Report (DP1 - DP4)
**Team #63 (Bell Labs)** | University of Lagos, Nigeria
**Project:** Security-Hardened AES-128 Peripheral (tqvp_belllabs_aes128)
**Date:** May 7, 2026

## 1. Executive Summary
This report documents the comprehensive 16-week engineering journey from the initial RTL design (DP1) to the final physical tape-out (DP4) of the `tqvp_belllabs_aes128` encryption core. The objective was to design a highly optimized, side-channel hardened AES-128 peripheral compatible with the TinyQV RISC-V SoC APB bus. Through aggressive PPA optimizations and physical design tuning, the final GDSII achieves 100% timing closure at the **64 MHz** system target (with a **294 MHz** silicon Fmax capability), **0.56 mm²** die area, and **0 DRC / 0 LVS** violations on the Sky130 node.

---

## 2. DP1: The Baseline Implementation
**Objective:** Establish a functionally correct, standard-compliant RTL baseline.

### Architecture
We selected an **Iterative Architecture** (10-cycle latency) over a fully unrolled or highly pipelined design to minimize extreme cell counts while meeting the TinyQV 64MHz APB bus timing.
- **Verification:** Passed all NIST SP800-38A Test Vectors.
- **Baseline Synthesis (Sky130):** 12,287 cells, 816 FFs, dominated by multiplexers (62.9%).

---

## 3. DP2: Power and Performance Optimization
**Objective:** Aggressively reduce dynamic power and maximize logic Fmax.

### The Clock Gating Strategy
To reduce the high switching power inherent in cryptographic S-boxes, we implemented **Multi-Bank Clock Gating**. We gated the 128-bit state registers during idle and non-encryption phases using Sky130 clock-gating cells (`sky130_fd_sc_hd__dlclkp`).
- **Power Reduction:** Dropped sequential switching power from 1.63 mW to **1.22 mW (a 25.1% reduction)**.
- **Timing Closure:** Initial Static Timing Analysis (STA) verified a maximum logic frequency of **328 MHz**, far exceeding the 64 MHz system requirement.

> [!NOTE]
> **Pipelining Trade-off:** We evaluated a 20-cycle pipelined architecture targeting 400+ MHz. However, we discarded it after realizing the physical bottleneck was the APB bus fan-out, not the AES datapath. The iterative design was definitively superior for our target constraints.

## 4. DP3: Security Hardening (SCA & Fault Resistance)
**Objective:** Secure the core against Side-Channel Analysis (SCA), specifically Differential Power Analysis (DPA) and timing attacks.

### Implemented Mitigations
1.  **First-Order Boolean Masking:** We integrated a 32-bit LFSR to generate random entropy, continuously masking the AES state registers to decouple power consumption from the actual plaintext/key data.
2.  **Dynamic Key Lockout:** Implemented a hardware lock that prevents the `KEY` registers from being read back over the APB bus (`0xDEADBEEF` is returned instead), preventing key extraction via JTAG/memory dumps.
3.  **Timing Invariance:** Ensured the engine runs in exactly 12 cycles regardless of the data, nullifying timing attacks.

### The Security Overhead
Adding Boolean masking required duplicating significant logic paths. 
- **Cell Count Increase:** Jumped from 12,303 to 13,361 (**+8.74% overhead**).
- **Wire Count Increase:** Jumped to 12,683 wires (**+12.05% overhead**).
This massive increase in wiring density would define the critical challenge of Phase 4.

## 5. DP4: Final Physical Design & Tape-out (The Climax)
**Objective:** Generate a DRC/LVS-clean GDSII layout while meeting the 64MHz system clock.

### The Routing Stall & The 750μm Pivot
The initial physical design targeted a 700μm x 700μm footprint. However, the high interconnect density generated by the DP3 Boolean Masking logic created severe pin congestion.
- **The Bottleneck:** The TritonRoute detailed router stalled on Iteration 45, freezing at **174 DRC violations** for over 48 hours. A chip with 174 violations has a 0% yield.
- **The Solution:** We executed an "Area-for-Reliability" pivot. We increased the `DIE_AREA` constraint to **750μm x 750μm** and relaxed the `PL_TARGET_DENSITY` to **0.35**. 
- **The Result:** The router converged in under 50 minutes, resolving 23,580 initial violations down to **0 DRC**.

### Final PPA & Sign-off Metrics
The design was signed off using the OpenLane flow with a strict `CLOCK_PERIOD: 15.62` (64 MHz) constraint.

| **Maximum Logic Fmax** | **294.1 MHz** | Extracted physical critical path is 3.40ns. |

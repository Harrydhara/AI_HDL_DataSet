# AI Strategy and Usage

## Tool Selection
Why did you choose specific AI tools?

We chose specific AI tools based on what programs we were most familiar with. Then from there we simply furthered our working knowledge with those tools, in terms of how we would prompt differently for each tool. 

## Prompting Techniques
What prompting strategies worked best?

Prompting that included the end goal were useful. Along with providing details on the exact repo and processor model with the peripheral template. Additionally we found that if we prompted with specific goals or few tasks required of the system, we saw less errors that the AI generation would make.

## Iteration Process
How did you refine AI-generated code?

We read through prompt responses and checked them against each others version of the code. We also took the generated code and read through them ourselves in order to make sure we knew what exactly was being created and changed. Comparing the results with our knowledge from classes was really useful. 

## AI Limitations Encountered
Where did AI fall short? How did you overcome limitations?

AI falls short when we overloaded the system with too many possibilities and things to check all at once. If we wanted specific answers, we had to give direct instructions on what we wanted and what limitations the code generation was allowed to have. 

## Learning from AI
What did AI teach you about hardware design?

So far we have learned that AI actually has a good grasp on hardware design. We saw the topics that we are learning about in class come up a couple of times through our prompting. This not helped us learn more about these topics, but also fortify what we had been learning.

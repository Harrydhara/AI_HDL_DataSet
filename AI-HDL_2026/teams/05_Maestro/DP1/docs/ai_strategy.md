# AI Strategy and Usage

## Tool Selection
We chose specific AI tools based on what programs we were most familiar with. All three of us use different AI chatbots like Gemini, ChatGPT, and Claude, so we stuck with whatever we were used to and cross-validated outputs generated from different sources.

## Prompting Techniques
In the beginning, when we were generating the first Verilog peripherals and testbenches, providing the given template and specifying the constraints/rules were the most useful prompting strategies. For example, we specified the exact repo (ttsky from TinyTapeout), processor (RISC-V), and external connections we wanted (ESP32 WiFi module) so the generated code would match exactly what we had in mind.

## Iteration Process
To refine AI-generated code, we read through prompt responses and checked them against other LLMs to validate the code. However, we did not end up changing any code generated by AI for challenge 1.

## AI Limitations Encountered
AI fell short in the beginning while coming up with project ideas. At first, we wanted to do a simple UART peripheral and AI was capable in generating code. Then, we decided to pivot to a more useful application, notably traffic controlling interfacing an ESP32 WiFi module. AI helped us realize that we needed to switch from UART to SPI communication, but we were the ones coming up with these original ideas. AI could have given us some project ideas, but we were the ones deciding to connect specific modules for practical applications.

## Learning from AI
AI taught us that hardware design can be simplified and introduced to beginners with little knowledge of tapeout and peripheral design if they know the right questions to ask. AI reminded us that knowing the fundamentals of coding, processors, communication protocols, and design verification are important to easily and successfully generate a working design with AI. Without knowing the fundamentals from previous knowledge in the field, the learning curve is very steep and people can get stuck at the beginning just from the shear amount of information to take in. However, the most important lesson for us was that AI can only accelerate the process through code generation, but it can only do so through giving the correct prompts and constraints.

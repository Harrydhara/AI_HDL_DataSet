## About the Competition

The **AI Hardware Design League (AI-HDL)** is an international competition organized by the **University of Arizona Center for Semiconductor Manufacturing**. It challenges student teams to design digital hardware using AI and Large Language Models (LLMs) — no prior hardware experience required.

Teams follow a complete **RTL-to-GDSII flow**, working on a shared RISC-V base design (TinyQV) and extending it with custom peripherals. The competition runs over four progressive phases, with the top teams earning actual chip tapeout through the Tiny Tapeout shuttle.

| Phase | Topic | Period |
|-------|-------|--------|
| Design Phase 1 | Module Implementation & Integration | Jan 15 – Jan 28, 2026 |
| Design Phase 2 | PPA Optimization | Feb 12 – Feb 26, 2026 |
| Design Phase 3 | Security & Vulnerability Hardening | Mar 12 – Mar 26, 2026 |
| Design Phase 4 | RTL-to-GDSII Tapeout | Apr 9 – Apr 23, 2026 |

## About the Team

**ICLabHUST1** is a team of undergraduate students from the **IC Design Lab** at Hanoi University of Science and Technology (HUST), one of Vietnam's leading technical universities.

We joined AI-HDL 2025-2026 to push the boundaries of how AI tools can be applied to hardware design — from writing and refining RTL code to optimization, verification, and physical implementation. Our team brings backgrounds in digital design, computer architecture, and embedded systems, and we see this competition as a real-world proving ground for AI-assisted chip design.

Our goals for this competition:
- Develop practical skills in VLSI design and the RTL-to-GDSII flow
- Build effective workflows for using LLMs in hardware engineering
- Contribute quality open-source peripheral designs for the RISC-V community
- Represent HUST at the international level
